PREMISE

For the Fans is a feature-length, ensemble comedy following a group of six spectators in real-time at a women's March Madness game, all of whom are pursuing their own, secret agendas.

Weaver and Dean find themselves seated next to one another. Dean tells Weaver should be so excited: her mother is the team's coach, and her big sister, is a star player! So why did Weaver tell him she hopes her mother and sister fail? Why is she even here? Dean really, really needs Bridgetown to win: how is he supposed to turbo charge the team's home court advantage without alienating Weaver?

Erin is the iron-fisted leader of Bridgetown U's pep band with dreams of stardom. It all seems to be happening when pop star Salem Boatright makes a surprise appearance at the game, but her idiot, ex-boyfriend, DJ Greg, is stealing her musical spotlight with his AI music.
